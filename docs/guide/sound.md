# Sound and appearance

Open **Settings** to adjust playback and personalize the interface.

## Equalizer and effects

Choose an equalizer preset or move the frequency controls for a custom sound. The available effects include radio-style filtering and reverberation. Reset controls are available in the sound settings. Large boosts can cause distortion depending on the source and output device.

## Playback

Adjust playback speed and crossfade duration in the sound settings. Crossfade mixes the end of one track with the beginning of the next when both tracks support the transition.

## Appearance

Choose the app’s main accent color in **Settings → Personalization**. The secondary accent is generated automatically from the selected color. You can also turn interface glow effects off or on. The setting is remembered locally.

## Resume playback

When **Resume playback on launch** is enabled, Veloly restores the last track, queue, and position where supported.
