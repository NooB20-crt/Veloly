# Using the player

## Search and discovery

Use the search field to find songs, artists, and albums from the available online sources. Open an artist or album to browse its tracks. Online search and streaming need an internet connection.

## Playback controls

The player bar provides play/pause, previous and next, shuffle, repeat, volume, lyrics, queue, sleep timer, output device, and equalizer controls. Some controls depend on the platform or selected audio source.

## Queue

Choose **Add to queue** to put a song near the front of the queue. Drag the handle beside a **Next Up** track to change its order. The current track cannot be moved as a queued item.

## Lyrics

Open lyrics from the player bar. Available synchronized lyrics follow playback; select a line to seek to that point when timing data is available. See the [lyrics guide](./lyrics).

## Keyboard shortcuts

Available shortcuts are listed in **Settings → Keyboard shortcuts**. They can vary by platform and can be changed there.
